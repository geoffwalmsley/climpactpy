{% load i18n ui help %}{% comment %} Quality's checks as a rail and the selected check's detail wide beside it (#264, the Quality board; a list and a detail pane since #202, #235). The rail: "Look wrong" and "Worth a look", each check a row with its variable, a small chart by calendar month and its count. Each row is a link to ``?show=``, so selecting works without JavaScript and can be linked; the server shows that check's detail, the first by default, and every other detail is in the page, hidden. With JavaScript (qc.js) a row swaps the detail in place and announces its title. Side by side from a 60rem container; narrower the rail comes first and the detail after it, with "Back to the list". The third section, "Possible breaks" (#365, the QualityBreaks board), after the checks: its note is the station's answer in a sentence (breaks.BreaksReview.sentence), then a row per series tested, with a timeline of its break dates in place of the month bars and their number; each series' detail is stations/_breaks_detail.html, selected as a check's.
