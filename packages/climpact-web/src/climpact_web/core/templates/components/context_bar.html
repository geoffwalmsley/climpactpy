{% load i18n static ui %}{% comment %} The context bar (#227, the workspace design of #230): under the app's header on every workspace and station page, across the window on the surface, over a hairline. In order: a breadcrumb (workspace / station) with the previous and next item at its end; the title (the page's h1, or a line above a page's own h1 when ``heading`` is false); one mono line of provenance (coordinates, period, base period, settings, version); the page's actions at the end of the title's row; then the tabs and anything under them (the Inputs bar). It stays in view as the page scrolls (sticky), so include it as a direct child of the page's block; js/context-bar.js keeps --context-bar-height to its height, which the page's scroll padding uses so focus never goes under it (WCAG 2.4.11). On phones (below 40rem) the title and a ⋯ menu share one line, the actions are in that menu (a
disclosure; the script closes it on Escape and on a click elsewhere), the provenance line keeps only its parts marked ``phone``, and only the tabs and what is under them stay in view. Parameters: title; heading (true: the title is the h1); provenance (a list of {text, phone}); breadcrumb (optional, a list of {label, url}: the last one, without a url, is the page); step_previous and step_next (optional, {label, url}: the previous and next station); actions (optional, a template with the page's actions); tabs (optional, the sections as components/workspace_tabs.html takes them) and tabs_label (their navigation's name); below (optional, a template under the tabs, such as the Inputs bar); static (true: in place, not sticky and not across the window, for /patterns/). {% endcomment %}
