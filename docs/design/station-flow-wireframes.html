Decisions (16 November 2026)

  1. A. Workspace navigation: tabs. Sections are tabs under the station header, each with a status dot. The five-step stepper is removed (#179).
  2. B. Quality verdict: three levels, and the user can always go on. Only the "look wrong" checks set the level; values that are unusual but may be real are counted in the text and never change it (#174).
    • Green, "Ready to use": nothing looks wrong. Primary: "Use this data".
    • Amber, "Usable. N values look wrong": up to 0.1% of the values checked look wrong (about 88 in an 80-year record). Primary: "Use this data"; secondary: "Upload a corrected file".
    • Red, "Fix the file first": more than 0.1% look wrong, or more than 10% of years are too incomplete for annual values. Primary: "Upload a corrected file"; secondary: "Use this data anyway".
    A base period with no usable data is already rejected by the station details form, so it never reaches this screen. Both thresholds can be tuned in #174 against the QC stress inputs (reference/make_qc_stress.py). No per-row "checked" state for now.
  3. C. Themes: Heat, Cold, Rain, Drought, then an "Also measured" strip (#175).
    • Heat: SU, TR, TXge30, TXge35, TXx, TNx, TX90p, TN90p, TX95t, WSDI, WSDId, TXdTNd, and the TX90 and EHF heatwave indices.
    • Cold: FD, TNlt2, TNltm2, TNltm20, ID, TNn, TXn, TX10p, TN10p, CSDI, CSDId, TXbdTNbd.
    • Rain: PRCPTOT, SDII, R10mm, R20mm, Rnnmm, Rx1day, Rx5day, Rxdday, CWD, R95p, R99p, R95pTOT, R99pTOT.
    • Drought: SPI, SPEI and CDD (consecutive dry days).
    • Also measured, one compact line each:
      • Averages: TXm, TNm, TMm, TXgt50p and DTR, the daily temperature range. Averages aren't extremes, so they fit neither Heat nor Cold; mean temperature feeds the one-sentence summary.
      • Growing season and degree days: GSL, GDDgrow, HDDheat, CDDcold, TMge5, TMlt5, TMge10, TMlt10. These come from the station's temperatures; no crop or energy data is needed.
      • Your own index, with its definition (for example "TN > 0"). It could measure anything, so it isn't themed.
  4. D. Speculative calculation: on in both modes, including climpact.app (#178).
  5. E. drawer component: approved. A side panel for section help. It isn't modal: it sits beside the content on wide screens and below it on phones. Escape closes it and focus returns to the ⓘ (#177).
